## 2. Topics Covered

### A. Python Data Types

- Integer (`int`)
- Float (`float`)
- String (`str`)
- Boolean (`bool`)
- Complex (`complex`)
- Introduction to List, Tuple, Set and Dictionary.

### B. Arithmetic Operators

- Addition (`+`)
- Subtraction (`-`)
- Multiplication (`*`)
- Division (`/`)
- Floor Division (`//`)
- Modulus (`%`)
- Exponentiation (`**`)

### C. Comparison Operators

- Equal to (`==`)
- Not equal to (`!=`)
- Greater than (`>`)
- Less than (`<`)
- Greater than or equal (`>=`)
- Less than or equal (`<=`)

### D. Logical Operators

- AND
- OR
- NOT

### E. Assignment Operators

- `=`
- `+=`
- `-=`
- `*=`
- `/=`
- `//=`
- `%=`
- `**=`

### F. Type Conversion

**Implicit Conversion:** Automatic conversion performed by Python.

**Explicit Conversion:** Manual conversion using functions such as:
- `int()`
- `float()`
- `str()`
- `bool()`
